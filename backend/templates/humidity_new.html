Sensor Mechanics

The DHT22 (AM2302) uses a capacitive humidity sensor to measure the moisture content in the surrounding air.

  • ✅ Range: 0–100% RH
  • ✅ Accuracy: ±2–5% RH
  • ✅ Resolution: 0.1%
  • ✅ Stable long-term operation
