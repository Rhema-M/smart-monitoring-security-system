How it Works

The LDR (Light Dependent Resistor) works by decreasing its resistance as the intensity of light increases. This change is converted into a percentage for easier monitoring.

  • ✅ Sensitivity: High
  • ✅ Range: 0% (Total Dark) to 100% (Direct Light)
  • ✅ Response Time: < 30ms
  • ✅ Application: Day/Night Detection
Light Zones
< 20% — Dark
20–70% — Dim / Indoor
> 70% — Bright
