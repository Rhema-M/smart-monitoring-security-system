How it Works

The DHT22 sensor measures ambient temperature by sensing the changes in electrical resistance of a moisture-holding substrate.

  • ✅ Range: -40°C to 80°C
  • ✅ Accuracy: ±0.5°C
  • ✅ Sampling Rate: 0.5Hz (Every 2 seconds)
  • ✅ Low power consumption (3.3V – 5.5V)
Temperature Zones

18–28°C — Comfortable
28–35°C — Warm
> 35°C — Hot
